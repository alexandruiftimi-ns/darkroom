# Trust / Corporate

**Idea:** competence you can verify. Calm, precise, evidence-led; nothing that looks like a trick.
**Use for:** finance, insurance, legal, medical, B2B services, public sector, consultancies.

## Colour — disciplined
Light ground, deep ink, one institutional accent (navy, green, teal, oxblood — from the brand), semantic colours for status. High contrast.

## Type
IBM Plex Sans/Serif, Source Sans 3 / Source Serif 4, Public Sans, Libre Franklin, Inter. Serif headings + sans text reads established; sans-only reads modern-institutional. Tabular numerals for figures.
Never: trendy display faces, thin grey body text.

## Layout
Clear IA, task-first navigation ("Open an account", "Find a doctor"), short pages with clear next steps, real people and credentials, accessible tables.

## Imagery
Real team and places, documentary style; diagrams that explain; no stock handshake.

## Motion
Minimal: state feedback, disclosure animations ≤ 200 ms.

## Switches
eyebrow SPARING · tracked-caps OFF · index-numerals SPARING (processes) · hairlines SPARING · cards SPARING · glass OFF · gradients OFF · mono-labels OFF · centred-layout OFF · marquee OFF · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery SPARING · script-lettering OFF

## Must-have
Regulatory disclosures, licence numbers, risk warnings where required, accessible authentication (WCAG 3.3.8), consistent help (3.2.6), accessibility statement (EU).

## Never
Invented awards/ratings, pressure tactics, hidden fees, unverifiable claims.

## Image prompt seed
"calm trustworthy professional-services website, light ground, deep navy ink, documentary photograph of real team at work, clear task-first navigation, serif headings, precise grid, no stock clichés"
