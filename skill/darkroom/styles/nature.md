# Nature / Organic

**Idea:** the place breathes. Big landscapes, real textures, unhurried pace; the visitor slows down.
**Use for:** outdoor brands, parks, farms, wineries, wellness retreats, eco products, expeditions.

## Colour — disciplined (earth)
Palette sampled from the actual land: moss, lichen, slate, river, bark, sky at a specific hour. Avoid the generic cream + terracotta; pick the subject's real colours. One accent (often a flower, sky or water colour).

## Type
Fraunces (soft, low "wonk"), Lora, Young Serif, Newsreader + Nunito Sans, Work Sans, Karla. Or a humanist sans alone (Source Sans 3, Nunito Sans). Comfortable 17–19 px body.
Never: techy geometric display, condensed bold.

## Layout
Large full-bleed landscape frames, text panels set into calm areas of the photo, generous vertical rhythm; maps and seasons as real content.

## Imagery
Location photography at a named time of day and season; textures (wood, stone, water); people small in landscape; drone sparingly.

## Motion
Signature: slow multi-layer parallax of landscape (foreground/mid/sky, 4–16 px), gentle crossfades between seasons. Nothing fast.

## Switches
eyebrow SPARING · tracked-caps SPARING · index-numerals OFF · hairlines OFF · cards SPARING · glass OFF · gradients SPARING (sky only) · mono-labels SPARING (coordinates/altitudes if real) · centred-layout SPARING · marquee OFF · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery ON · script-lettering OFF

## Must-have
Real sustainability claims only (EU EmpCo rules on green claims); accessibility of maps; heavy image optimisation.

## Never
Stock leaves, generic "eco" green, vague "sustainable" claims without proof.

## Image prompt seed
"<landscape> at <time of day> in <season>, natural light, rich texture, layered depth for parallax, calm sky area for typography, palette of <sampled earth colours>, documentary not stock"
