# Catalog / Commerce

**Idea:** browsing should feel effortless and honest; products are compared quickly and bought without doubt.
**Use for:** e-commerce, product ranges, rentals, marketplaces, B2B catalogs.

## Colour — disciplined
Light neutral ground almost always (products photograph best on it); one accent for add-to-cart/primary; sale/price colours tested for contrast; never colour alone for stock status.

## Type
One sans family: Manrope, Inter, Instrument Sans, Figtree, DM Sans. Optional display face for campaign banners only. **Tabular numerals for prices.** Product titles 15–18 px, prices equal or more prominent.

## Layout
Consistent product grid (same image ratio for all), filters that respond instantly and live in the URL, product page: gallery left, decision column right (title, price, variant, CTA, delivery/returns), details below.

## Imagery
Consistent background, lighting and crop across all products; one lifestyle image per product max on the grid; zoom on detail pages.

## Motion
Signature: image swap on hover (desktop), quick filter feedback, add-to-cart confirmation. No decorative motion on the grid.

## Switches
eyebrow OFF · tracked-caps SPARING · index-numerals OFF · hairlines SPARING · cards ON (product tiles, uniform by design) · glass OFF · gradients OFF · mono-labels OFF · centred-layout OFF · marquee SPARING (shipping notice) · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery SPARING · script-lettering OFF

## Must-have (EU)
Final price incl. VAT; unit price where required; delivery costs before checkout; "was" price = lowest of last 30 days; returns/withdrawal info; accessible checkout (EAA); no pre-ticked add-ons; cookie consent with equal reject.

## Never
Fake scarcity, resetting countdowns, hidden fees, confirmshaming ("No thanks, I hate saving"), forced account creation without need.

## Image prompt seed
"clean e-commerce category page, uniform product photographs on <neutral> background, consistent 4:5 crops, clear price typography with tabular numbers, filter bar, one <accent> add-to-cart colour"
