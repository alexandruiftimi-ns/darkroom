# Artsy / Playful

**Idea:** the site is itself a creative piece — surprising, colourful, interactive — while the content stays findable.
**Use for:** creative studios, illustrators, festivals, zines, music artists, experimental launches.

## Colour — expressive
Colour is the material: several saturated hues, colour-blocking, clashing on purpose. Still: one dominant per viewport, text contrast passes, CTA style consistent, a resting neutral somewhere.

## Type
Expressive display: Bricolage Grotesque, Syne, Gloock, Rubik (variable), Unbounded, variable fonts with animated axes. Text: Inter, Work Sans, DM Sans. Up to three families (display, text, utility); the display face may use several widths/weights of itself.
Never: three expressive faces; unreadable body type.

## Layout
Broken grids, overlaps, stickers/cut-outs, scale contrast; a stable nav and clear content index so play doesn't hide information.

## Imagery
Illustration, collage, 3D toys, hand-made textures, the artist's own work.

## Motion
Signature: playful cursor interaction, draggable objects (with keyboard alternative — WCAG 2.5.7), colour-field shifts, variable-font axes on hover. Springy easing allowed.

## Switches
eyebrow SPARING · tracked-caps SPARING · index-numerals SPARING · hairlines SPARING · cards SPARING · glass SPARING · gradients ON (chosen, not default violet) · mono-labels SPARING · centred-layout ON · marquee ON (one) · custom-cursor ON (with normal cursor fallback) · full-bleed-imagery ON · script-lettering ON (hand lettering as art)

## Must-have
Reduced-motion version; keyboard alternative for every drag/cursor game; content readable without the toys.

## Never
Play that blocks navigation, essential info only inside an interaction, motion that flashes.

## Image prompt seed
"playful experimental creative-studio website, bold clashing colour blocks of <hues>, expressive <display description> type at huge scale, collage cut-outs and stickers, broken grid but clear navigation"
