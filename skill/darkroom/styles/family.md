# Family / Friendly

**Idea:** warm, safe and easy for everyone — tired parents on a phone, grandparents, kids. Clarity is kindness.
**Use for:** kids' products, schools, nurseries, community centres, family health, local services.

## Colour — expressive (soft)
Friendly saturated-but-soft hues for section fields, a calm neutral ground, high text contrast (aim beyond AA for body). Colour never the only signal.

## Type
Text: Atkinson Hyperlegible, Lexend, Nunito, Figtree (legibility first). Display: Fredoka, Baloo 2, Nunito (heavy), Bricolage Grotesque (rounded feel). 18 px+ body, short lines.
Never: thin weights, all-caps paragraphs, condensed faces.

## Layout
One column on mobile, big obvious buttons (≥ 44 px), plain-language headings that answer questions ("When are you open?"), essential info first (times, prices, contact, location).

## Imagery
Real people from the community (with consent), bright natural light, illustration with rounded shapes; no stock "perfect family".

## Motion
Signature: gentle press feedback, small celebratory moments after an action. No surprises, no autoplay.

## Switches
eyebrow OFF · tracked-caps OFF · index-numerals SPARING (real steps) · hairlines OFF · cards ON (soft, rounded, varied colour) · glass OFF · gradients SPARING · mono-labels OFF · centred-layout SPARING · marquee OFF · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery SPARING · script-lettering SPARING

## Must-have
Consent for photos of children; plain-language privacy notice; accessible forms; clear prices incl. VAT; consistent help location (WCAG 3.2.6).

## Never
Dark patterns, guilt copy, pressure sales, tiny text, cartoonish type on serious info (health, safety).

## Image prompt seed
"friendly family-service website, soft rounded shapes, warm <hues> colour sections, large clear headings, real diverse families in natural light, big obvious buttons, calm and welcoming"
