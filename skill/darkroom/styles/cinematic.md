# Cinematic

**Idea:** the site is a sequence of shots. Camera, light and one hero subject carry the story; the interface is the subtitle.
**Use for:** product launches, hardware, automotive, film/game, architecture reveals. **Not for:** content-heavy or task UIs.

## Colour — disciplined
Dark, hue-tinted ground (not #000); UI in neutrals + one secondary family; one accent for the primary action. Saturation lives in the footage/grade. Example roles: ground `#080B13`, surface `#101827`, ink `#F3F6FB`, ink-2 `#9BA9BC`, secondary `#7FA8D8`, accent `#FF6D61` (NorthShutter) — derive yours from the subject's light.

## Type
Display: light/regular, wide or neutral grotesk at large size — Sora, Manrope, Archivo (wdth axis), Space Grotesk, Syne (sparingly). Text: Inter, Manrope. H1 5–7vw on desktop, weight 200–400, tight leading 0.9–1.0.
Never: condensed bold "trailer" type everywhere, script fonts as UI.

## Layout
Full-bleed frames, one subject at 45–65 % of width, constant copy anchor (often left 36 %), header quiet and constant. One section ≈ one shot; pinned scenes for transformations.

## Imagery
Controlled light direction, depth layers (fore/mid/back), consistent grade across sections, product silhouette lock, wet reflections/haze used with purpose.

## Motion
Signature: scrubbed object rotation, lens/portal dive, light sweep. Scroll scrubs linear, everything reversible. One motion per shot.

## Switches
eyebrow SPARING (title-card style) · tracked-caps SPARING · index-numerals SPARING (only as chapter progress) · hairlines SPARING · cards OFF · glass OFF · gradients OFF (light gradients only inside imagery) · mono-labels OFF · centred-layout SPARING · marquee OFF · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery ON · script-lettering OFF

## Must-have
Poster frame visible before heavy media; reduced-motion poster per scene; real DOM text over imagery with local scrims.

## Never
Autoplay sound, scroll-jacking, a different camera language per section, more than one glowing thing at a time.

## Image prompt seed
"cinematic product film still, anamorphic feel, single key light from <dir>, deep shadows, controlled reflections, colour grade <ground/secondary/accent words>, generous dark negative space on the left for typography"
