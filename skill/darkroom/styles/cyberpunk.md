# Cyberpunk

**Idea:** neon noir — the city at night, light on wet surfaces, technology that feels close and slightly dangerous. Atmosphere in media, discipline in UI.
**Use for:** games, gaming hardware, tech events, nightlife, sci-fi media. **Not for:** healthcare, finance, family.

## Colour — disciplined UI, saturated media
Near-black blue/violet ground; UI ink cool white; **one** UI accent (coral, cyan or acid — pick one); magenta/cyan/orange neon lives inside imagery. The classic failure is six neon UI colours.

## Type
Display: Chakra Petch, Oxanium, Rajdhani, Space Grotesk, Sora (wide light reads "premium future"). Text: Inter, IBM Plex Sans. Utility mono (JetBrains Mono, IBM Plex Mono) **ON for real HUD data only** (coordinates, distances, mission timers).
Never: Orbitron for everything, glitch text on body copy, scanlines over text.

## Layout
Cinematic frames with a constant copy anchor; contextual HUD panels (one at a time, tied to the scene); grid can be visible as structure (hairlines OK).

## Imagery
Night cities, rain, reflections, practical neon, haze, palm/skyline silhouettes, devices with emissive accents. Consistent grade.

## Motion
Signature: light sweep, route/path drawing, HUD element appear tied to scene; glitch/chromatic aberration only on media, briefly. No constant flicker (WCAG 2.3.1).

## Switches
eyebrow ON (title-card) · tracked-caps ON (short) · index-numerals ON (chapter/progress) · hairlines ON · cards SPARING (HUD panels only) · glass SPARING (one HUD style) · gradients SPARING (inside media) · mono-labels SPARING (real data) · centred-layout SPARING · marquee OFF · custom-cursor SPARING · full-bleed-imagery ON · script-lettering SPARING (neon signage inside art only)

## Must-have
Contrast of small text over glowing media; reduced-motion removes flicker/glitch; HUD readable on mobile or removed.

## Never
Random fake CPU graphs, rainbow HUD, glow on every border, matrix rain.

## Image prompt seed
"neon noir night city, rain-slick reflective street, practical magenta and cyan signage, haze, cool blue shadows, single warm accent light, cinematic depth, calm dark area on the left for typography, no readable text"
