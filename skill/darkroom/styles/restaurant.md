# Restaurant / Hospitality

**Idea:** appetite and place. The visitor must *taste* the food and *find* the door within seconds.
**Use for:** restaurants, bars, cafés, bakeries, food halls. Choose a sub-mood: fine dining (luxury-leaning), bistro (warm editorial), street/casual (bold-leaning).

## Colour — from the venue
Take colours from the room, tiles, signage, ceramics, the food itself. Fine dining → disciplined/mono; casual → expressive allowed.

## Type
Fine dining: Cormorant Garamond, Gloock + Jost/Karla. Bistro: Fraunces, DM Serif Display + DM Sans, Work Sans. Casual: Bricolage Grotesque, Anton + Inter. If the venue's sign has a typeface, echo it in the display role.
Never: script fonts for menu items, more than three families, tiny menu text.

## Layout
First viewport: food or room image + name + **Reserve** + **hours/address** (or one tap away). Menu as **HTML text** (not PDF; PDF only as optional download). Sections: menu, story (short), the room, visit (map, transport, hours), private events, contact.

## Imagery
Real dishes, real room, real people at work; warm practical light; overhead and 45° shots; no stock food.

## Motion
Signature: dish close-up reveal / slow image crossfade. Nothing that slows the phone user looking for hours.

## Switches
eyebrow SPARING · tracked-caps SPARING · index-numerals OFF · hairlines SPARING (menu sections) · cards OFF · glass OFF · gradients OFF · mono-labels OFF · centred-layout SPARING · marquee OFF · custom-cursor OFF · full-bleed-imagery ON · script-lettering SPARING (if it's the sign)

## Must-have
Hours, address, phone, reservation link above the fold on mobile; menu with prices incl. VAT (EU) and allergen information path; accessible map alternative (text directions); fast on mobile data.

## Never
PDF-only menu, autoplay music, splash intro video, hours hidden in a footer image.

## Image prompt seed
"<venue mood> restaurant website, hero photograph of <signature dish> in warm practical light on <surface/tile from the venue>, restaurant name in <type description>, visible reserve button and opening hours, appetising, real not stock"
