We make plates for kitchens that are used, not photographed. Every glaze is mixed here, every rim is checked by hand, and nothing leaves the studio until it has held a real meal.

How a plate is made

  1. 01 Throw

    Clay from Hordaland, thrown on the wheel in runs of forty.

  2. 02 Trim

    Feet and rims trimmed when leather-hard.

  3. 03 Glaze

    Dipped by hand in glazes mixed in the studio.

  4. 04 Fire

    Two firings, the second to 1280 °C.
