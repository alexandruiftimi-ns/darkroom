# Surfing (Framer template) — good

**Thesis in one line:** A surf school sold by big, bright surf photographs and one shouted wordmark with an orange asterisk.

**Split:** design good · build mixed · technique mixed — energetic photo-led layout; white-on-sky contrast, one long block.

**First frame:** full-bleed photo of a surfer on a blue wave; "SURF*IING" in heavy white grotesk spans the top, the asterisk in orange with an orange scribble under it; a star rating and a two-line promise centred near the bottom. White nav bar with an orange "Contact Us" pill.

## What works & why

- **Real action photography as section breaks** — full-bleed surf shots between content blocks keep the energy up → the subject is the image → rule: alternate content with one big real photo.
- **One accent mark with a story** — the orange asterisk and scribble recur in the logo, buttons and ticker → one colour, one device → rule: give the accent one shape and reuse it.
- **Beach-sign ticker** — the marquee uses warning-sign copy from the beach, not client logos → the device comes from the subject → rule: fill a marquee with words from the place.
- **Wordmark opens and closes** — the same giant name at the top and bottom → wordmark-bookend → rule: bookend a photo-led page with the name.

## What fails & why

- **White text on bright sky** (medium) — the sub-line and rating sit on pale blue and fail contrast → scrim or dark copy → rule: place copy on the darkest part of the photo or add a gradient scrim.
- **Template badges float over content** (medium, demo only) — remix and builder badges cover the bottom-right corner → rule: remove builder badges before launch.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let big, bright, real photos carry a sports or travel brand.
- Don't: put light copy on light sky.

## Never copy

Logo, photography, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
