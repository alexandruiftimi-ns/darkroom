## What works & why
- **One colour field per chapter, cream ink constant** → principle: cadence hook "colour-field change" (attention.md); expressive mode, one dominant per viewport (color.md) → rule: in an expressive palette keep the ink colour fixed so the fields can change freely.
- **Task bar of four real restaurant actions at the hero's foot** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: a restaurant's first frame offers its actual tasks (book, order, find), not "Learn more".
- **Daily specials as round plates on wood, same crop and label stack** → principle: related items in one row, labels in a fixed position (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: shoot comparable dishes from one angle and frame them identically.

## Transferable rules
- Do: let a wavy or characterful display face carry the brand once per section; keep body in a calm rounded sans.
- Don't: scatter big-number stat circles across a page (tells.md stat banner) — here they float in four corners and make the eye orbit (reading-paths.md rule 5).

**Open question:** 3 families, a 503-word jobs block on the home page and fonts loaded before consent (audit) hold confidence at medium.
