## What works & why

- **Before/after slider on the same screen** → show, don't claim (INDEX, product-in-scene) → rule: when the product is "better design", let the visitor drag between worse and better.
- **One action repeated** (join the waitlist) top, hero and close → one job per page → rule: a pre-launch page has one form, asked three times.
- **Warm off-white and black, no accent colour** → restraint (references/colour) → rule: a tool for designers lets its examples bring the colour.
- **Integration row (the tools it plugs into) on a black band** → traceable proof → rule: name the tools you work with instead of inventing customer logos.

## What fails & why

- **The hero uses the exact devices it mocks** (pill badge, italic serif accent word) (medium) → generated-design tells (tells.md) → rule: a page that sells taste needs one signature move of its own.

## Transferable rules

- Do: prove a quality claim with an interactive comparison.
- Don't: lean on the pill-badge-plus-italic-word hero when the product is "not slop".

## Never copy

Logo, UI, render, layout and copy. Learn the principle only.
