# Igloo Inc — good

**Thesis in one line:** A company told as one continuous 3D world where the brand mark (an igloo) is the scene itself.

**First frame:** cold grey void; a wireframe igloo with a glowing opening sits at centre, surrounded by numbered vertex lines. No copy in the first frame; the object is the brand.

## What works & why

- **The logo becomes the world** → the signature device comes from the subject (LESSONS) → rule: if the brand has a literal mark, build the hero scene from it.
- **Monochrome cold grey with one light source** → restraint carries 3D (cinematic pack) → rule: a 3D scene reads as premium when the palette is nearly colourless and lighting does the work.
- **Loading, navigation and story live in the same scene** → continuity (motion-craft.md) → rule: one camera, one world; chapters are camera stops, not page cuts.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **No text and no heading in the page markup** (high for a business site) → name who/what in the first frame (tells.md) → rule: keep a live h1 and a one-line claim in the HTML under the scene.

## Transferable rules

- Do: build the scene from the brand mark; camera stops as chapters.
- Don't: ship a business site whose words exist only inside WebGL.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
