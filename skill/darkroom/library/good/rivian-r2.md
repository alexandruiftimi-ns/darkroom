# Rivian R2 — good

**Thesis in one line:** An EV feature tour told in big product photography, tactile close-ups and short chapter claims.

**First frame:** a large hero image area under the nav (loads in), then chapter claims like "…for days" over the car outdoors.

## What works & why

- **Tactile close-ups (a hand on a control)** → show the experience → rule: one close-up of a hand using the product sells more than a spec.
- **A charging map as its own chapter** → answer the real objection → rule: give the buyer's biggest worry a full chapter.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Hero area is an empty grey box while it loads** (low–medium) → first frame (tells.md) → rule: give the hero a poster image.

## Transferable rules

- Do: hand-on-product close-ups; an objection chapter.
- Don't: leave the hero blank while video loads.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
