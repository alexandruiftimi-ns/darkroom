## What works & why

- **Short claims between strong images** → word budgets → rule: one sentence per screen is enough when the image is strong.
- **Services as a clean line-icon grid on a light band** → contrast change marks a chapter → rule: switch ground (dark → light) for the practical section.
- **Vertical monogram as the closing image** → close on the wordmark (LESSONS).

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Desktop page slightly wider than the screen (53 px)** (medium: sideways wobble) → overflow check → rule: test 1440 and 390 for horizontal scroll.

## Transferable rules

- Do: dark/light chapter switch; monogram closer.
- Don't: let one wide element push the page sideways.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
