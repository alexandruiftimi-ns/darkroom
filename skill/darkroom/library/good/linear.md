## What works & why

- **Real product screens as every section image** → show the real task (LESSONS) → rule: in SaaS, screenshots beat illustrations.
- **Light page (about 200 KB) with small, purposeful motion** → motion budget → rule: subtle reveals on real UI feel more premium than big effects.
- **Uses Inter, the most common "AI" font, and still looks designed** → execution beats font choice → rule: spacing, contrast and real content matter more than the typeface.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Dim grey secondary text** (low–medium) → contrast → rule: keep secondary text ≥ 4.5:1 on dark.

## Transferable rules

- Do: real UI as section images; quiet motion.
- Don't: fade secondary text too far on black.
