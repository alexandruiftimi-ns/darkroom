## What works & why

- **A strict logo grid on black, equal cells** → comparable items share one anatomy (LESSONS) → rule: put logos in an even grid with hairlines, never a scatter.
- **Project cards are mostly screenshot** → show the thing sold → rule: in a portfolio the work is 80 % of each card.
- **Ends on BSMNT.26 at full width with the nav under it** → close on the wordmark (LESSONS).

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Many looping animations ignore reduced motion** (medium) → motion.md → rule: patterned background loops stop for reduced motion.

## Transferable rules

- Do: equal-cell logo grid; screenshot-led cards; wordmark closer.
- Don't: keep decorative loops for reduced-motion visitors.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
