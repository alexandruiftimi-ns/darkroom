## What works & why

- **One object, lit like a studio shot, on a matching haze** → hero-media.md, edge colour = ground → rule: tint the backdrop from the product's own material so the object and page read as one picture.
- **Specs as three big numbers with tiny caps labels** → one value per viewport (sincro) → rule: a hardware launch earns trust with three checkable numbers, not adjectives.
- **Nav items are the 3D scenes (inspect, explode, place)** → navigation names the experience → rule: when the page is a stage, name the stops after what the product does in each.

## What fails & why

- **No phone page at all** (high) → mobile re-composition (references/surfaces) → rule: a phone gets at least a poster, the claim, the specs and the buy action.
- **Pale lilac text on lilac, five zones in the first view** (medium) → contrast and reading-zones checks → rule: one darker ink for body copy; merge copy into ≤ 3 zones.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let the product's material set the page colour.
- Don't: tell phone visitors to come back later.

## Never copy

Product model, logo, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
