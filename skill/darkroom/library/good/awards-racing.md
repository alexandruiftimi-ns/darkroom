# Awards Racing — good

**Thesis in one line:** A game launch sold like a movie poster: one massive condensed wordmark over two 3D characters and smoke.

**First frame:** dark smoke, two rendered characters facing each other, a turbine wheel below; AWARDS RACING in huge yellow condensed caps across the middle. Sound on/off sits at the corner.

## What works & why

- **Poster composition: characters, title, one prop** → display type and image as one object (LESSONS) → rule: for entertainment, compose the first frame as a key-art poster.
- **Sound is a choice up front** → sound is opt-in (LESSONS) → rule: offer sound, never force it.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **About 20 MB before you scroll** (medium on phones) → page weight lesson → rule: stream the 3D after the poster frame and keep the poster light.

## Transferable rules

- Do: key-art poster as the first frame.
- Don't: make phones download the whole world up front.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
