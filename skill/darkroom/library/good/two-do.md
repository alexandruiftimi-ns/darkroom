# Two-Do — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a couples' to-do app concept told as numbered "moments" beside a pinned, live phone prototype.
- **First frame:** two-line serif headline at ~3–35 % x / 28–48 % y with two italic words coloured peach and sage, body, a "phone is live" prompt and a demo button; right, a phone frame (screen blank at capture) with "Live prototype · Reset" below; olive-brown gradient ground.

## What works & why
- **Pinned phone driven by the chapters** ("Play on the phone", "Now playing · 05") → principle: one effect at a time, one focal point (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: for an app concept, pin one device and let each text chapter play exactly one state on it.
- **Colour roles stated in the copy** — sage and peach are the two people, gold is the agent, terracotta only when they meet → principle: name roles, not colours (color.md) → rule: give each accent one semantic owner and never use it as decoration.
- **Accessibility shown as design** — contrast ratios printed beside each accent, reduced-motion behaviour described → principle: WCAG 1.4.3 / 2.3.3 as visible quality (compliance.md) → rule: in a design-led concept, show the contrast and motion decisions, don't just claim them.
## Transferable rules
- Do: one device, chapters that drive it; accents with named owners.
- Don't: 4 families and 23 sizes — a strict colour-role system deserves an equally strict type system.

**Open question:** the phone screen is blank in every capture, so the core interaction is unverified; it is a designer's concept piece, not a shipping product — confidence medium.
