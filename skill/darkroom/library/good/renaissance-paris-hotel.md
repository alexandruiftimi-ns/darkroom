# Renaissance Paris concept (Olha Lazarieva) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a city hotel sold as a magazine spread about Paris — "Time" and "Memories" split across two photos.
- **First frame:** cream sheet inset on a street photo; left column: letter-spaced "unforgettable", bold "Time", a vintage car photo, the address; right column: street photo, split bold "Mem/ories", "SINCE 1872" tag; black BOOK A ROOM button top right; a dotted rule divides the columns.

## What works & why
- **Two-column spread with a dotted spine** → principle: copy anchor per column, related image + word together (reading-paths.md rules 1, 4) → rule: give each claim word its own photo and column instead of one hero for everything.
- **Sell the neighbourhood, not the room** (street, car, café awning) → principle: replace generic luxury with the subject's real context (tells.md "Why removal isn't enough") → rule: for city hotels, the first frame shows the street the guest will walk.
- **Address and phone as typographic elements** → principle: proof by concrete fact (attention.md "replace adjectives with one fact") → rule: let the real address do the trust work.
## Transferable rules
- Do: build the hero as an editorial spread with one weight contrast (bold vs. letter-spaced light).
- Don't: add the giant outlined "123" behind the photos — it crosses the focal image and adds a fourth layer.

**Open question:** the split word "Mem/ories" and the outlined numerals border on decoration; the booking control is a nav button only, not a date bar.
