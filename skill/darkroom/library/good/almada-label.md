## What works & why
- **Product rail: same cutout on the same pale grey, name left / price right on one baseline** → principle: related items in one row with labels in a fixed position (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: catalogue cards share photo treatment, crop and label grid so only the garment varies.
- **Monochrome UI, colour only in photos (accent 0.1 %)** → principle: separate UI palette from media palette; monochrome mode (color.md) → rule: when the product is the colour, keep chrome ink-on-paper.
- **Footer wordmark at full width** → principle: one focal point per viewport (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: end a quiet page on the brand mark at display scale instead of another block of links.

## Transferable rules
- Do: alternate paired lifestyle photography with a strict product rail — cadence by image scale, not motion (attention.md).
- Don't: set a thin white wordmark and grey meta text over light photos or grey grounds; it measured 1:1 and 3.7:1.

**Open question:** contrast failures (hero wordmark, material labels) and 4 font roles keep confidence at medium; nominee status fits the photography more than the UI.
