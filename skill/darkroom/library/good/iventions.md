# Iventions — good

**Thesis in one line:** An events agency that opens and closes on its giant wordmark, with each service chapter on its own pastel ground.

**First frame:** a huge IVENTIONS wordmark cropped at the bottom edge, a lavender shape behind a dark media card, "Step into the Spotlight" small at left. The eye reads the wordmark, then the card.

## What works & why

- **Giant wordmark cropped by the screen edge, used again at the very end** → close on the wordmark (LESSONS) → rule: bookend a brand page with the same cropped mark.
- **Numbered services, each with its own ground colour (Exhibits, Congresses)** → chapter colour (INDEX.md, By technique) → rule: give each chapter one ground colour so scrolling feels like turning pages.
- **Client names as big type, a testimonial with a real UEFA logo** → proof as type → rule: list clients as display type, not a logo soup.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Some sections run 300–390 words** (low–medium) → word budgets → rule: cut case text to a line per project; link out for detail.

## Transferable rules

- Do: cropped wordmark bookends; a ground colour per chapter.
- Don't: let case sections turn into essays.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
