## What works & why

- **The work is the motion: letters deform on scroll** → the signature device comes from the subject (LESSONS) → rule: if the product is type, animate the type, not boxes.
- **One cyan on white** → one accent (color.md) → rule: kinetic type needs a calm palette to stay readable.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **32 MB page** (medium on phones) → page weight lesson → rule: kinetic type should be live text and SVG, not video.
- **Long nearly empty white screens between moments** (low–medium) → cadence (attention.md) → rule: every screen of scroll should show something.

## Transferable rules

- Do: animate the letters themselves.
- Don't: pad the scroll with empty screens.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
