# Shōmei (film studio concept) — good

**Thesis in one line:** A film studio sold by one red and one condensed typeface at poster size, with film frames cut into the letters.

**Split:** design good · build mixed · technique good — strong type system; red-on-red copy hurts reading.

**First frame:** a red-graded footage loop fills the screen; a three-line condensed-caps claim sits bottom-left (2–50 % x, 58–92 % y); wordmark top-left, MENU top-right. Eye: the claim, then the moving image behind it.

## What works & why

- **Film frames placed across giant caps** → type-window (new technique) → rule: let the work and the title share one frame; the clip covers part of the word and the word still reads.
- **One family, one colour, two grounds** → colour discipline (references/colour) → rule: a single display face plus one brand hue can carry a whole studio site.
- **A client quote set as display type** → hierarchy by scale → rule: when proof is the point, set it at headline size instead of in a card.
- **The wordmark closes the page at full width** → wordmark-bookend (INDEX) → rule: end on the mark, with three links under it.

## What fails & why

- **Red claim over red-graded footage** (high) → contrast; text that needs the media (tells.md) → rule: when type and image share a hue, the type takes the cream, or a scrim darkens the image.
- **A long all-caps paragraph in the condensed face** (medium) → typography.md, display faces stop at headlines → rule: body copy in a text cut, sentence case.

## Transferable rules

- Do: build the page from one display face and one hue; vary scale, not style.
- Don't: put same-hue text on same-hue footage.

## Never copy

Name, logo, footage, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
