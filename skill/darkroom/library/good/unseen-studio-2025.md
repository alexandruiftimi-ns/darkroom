## What works & why

- **A mascot carries the loading moment** → the loader is part of the brand → rule: if you need a loader, make it a character, and keep it short.
- **An annual report as a playful world instead of a PDF** → format from the subject → rule: a studio's report can be an experience.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Slow on phones (about 14 s, measured by scrollytelling.ai)** (medium) → performance → rule: stream the world after the first chapter.

## Transferable rules

- Do: a mascot loader; report as a world.
- Don't: make phones wait for the whole world.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
