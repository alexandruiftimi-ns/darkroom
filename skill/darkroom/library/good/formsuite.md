## What works & why

- **Serif display over a grotesk system** → two families, two jobs (typography.md) → rule: one warm serif for promises, one neutral sans for everything else.
- **The real builder is the hero image** → show the product working (product-in-scene) → rule: a live, scrolling mock beats a static screenshot for a tool.
- **One deep-brown band in a white page** → a single dark chapter as a pause (colour.md) → rule: one tonal inversion mid-page resets attention.
- **Manifesto paragraph that inks in word by word with scroll** → scroll-linked reveal with a job → rule: use it once, on 25–60 words.
- **Faint stat cards and tags as small colour accents** → accent with an owner → rule: colour on proof, not on decoration.

## What fails & why

- **A 500-word FAQ block** (medium) → word budget → rule: collapse answers; show five questions.
- **Faint caps labels (≈ 4:1)** (medium) → contrast law → rule: small caps labels need ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Sticky nav awkward and a horizontal slide that drifts off-screen** (medium, per the thread) → rule: sticky elements must never overlap section content.

## Transferable rules

- Do: serif promise, live product mock, one dark band.
- Don't: let FAQ or labels drop below the reading budget.

## Never copy

Logo, product UI, photography, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
