- **Thesis in one line:** fair-trade chocolate snacks sold loud — heavy caps, chocolate brown, a lime action colour and melting drips.

## What works & why
- **Chocolate drips as the one brand device** — under the hero card, the quote label and the story panel → principle: one product-tied device instead of generic decoration (tells.md, Colour & surface) → rule: invent one device from the product itself and reuse it on 2–3 surfaces only.
- **Palette lifted from the packaging** — brown wrapper, lime tree logo, raspberry pink; lime is the action colour → principle: name roles, not colours (color.md) → rule: in packaged goods the UI colours are the pack colours, each with one role.
- **Identical bordered product cards** — caps name, unit · price line, then −/+ and Add to cart on one baseline → principle: comparable items aligned (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: every card keeps quantity and add-to-cart in the same row.
## Transferable rules
- Do: one drip-like device; pack colours as UI roles; one baseline for buy controls.
- Don't: set long testimonials in heavy display caps; don't use "!!!!" urgency.
