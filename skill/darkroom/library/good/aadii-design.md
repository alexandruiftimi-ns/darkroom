# Aadi Design Studio — good

**Thesis in one line:** A one-person brand studio that opens on two giant cream words with one orange letter, and files its work as a shelf of book spines.
**Split:** design good · build mixed · technique good — confident type and work shelf; 11 MB and heavy motion.

**First frame:** near-black ground; "Crafting / Brands" in a cream grotesk at about 240 px, staggered across the full width (y 25–70 %); one letter in orange, a glossy orange flame mark above the first line and a lightning bolt below the second; a bracketed one-sentence description bottom-left, a green-outline "Start a Project" button bottom-right, orange "Get in Touch" top-right. The eye: giant words → orange letter → bolt.

## What works & why

- **Giant two-word claim with one accent letter** → focal point by scale (references/craft) → rule: one colour change inside the headline beats a coloured headline.
- **Work as a shelf of vertical spines, one open at a time** → progressive disclosure → rule: list many projects as narrow spines and open one in full; the list reads as volume, the open one as depth.
- **Cream instead of white on black** → warm neutrals (references/colour) → rule: tint the "white" for a dark page.
- **Giant wordmark bookends the page** → wordmark-bookend (INDEX) → rule: close on the name, set as large as the opener.

## What fails & why

- **About 11 MB of images and video** (high) → weight budget → rule: compress case boards; no single image over 300 KB.
- **Page wider than the screen on desktop; grey small text on black** (medium) → overflow / contrast → rule: check horizontal overflow and ≥ 4.5:1 for all body text.
- **Heavy-handed entrances on every block** (medium) → same fade on every block (tells.md, Motion) → rule: animate the hero and the spines; let the rest arrive still.

## Transferable rules

- Do: one accent letter, one accent mark, one wordmark close.
- Don't: ship multi-megabyte case boards.

## Never copy

Logo, marks, portrait, project boards, layout and copy. Learn the principle only.
