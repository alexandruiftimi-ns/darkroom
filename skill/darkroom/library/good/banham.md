# Banham — good

**Thesis in one line:** a century-old locksmith sold as a heritage house — serif voice, monochrome craft photos, one booking action everywhere.

## What works & why
- **One serif family for wordmark, headings and body** → principle: tone must match the brand; 2-family budget (typography.md) → rule: a heritage trust brand can carry its whole voice in one serif superfamily plus a tiny utility sans.
- **Services as a 4-up row of B&W photos with centred serif labels beneath** → principle: related items in one row, labels in a fixed position (reading-paths.md rule 4); colour lives in media only (color.md) → rule: desaturate a mixed photo archive to make it one set.
- **Docked booking pill on every viewport, slate like the contact block** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6); one accent hue shared by CTAs (color.md) → rule: a single persistent conversion action in the brand's one UI accent beats repeated mid-page buttons.

## Transferable rules
- Do: give a heritage brand one serif voice and one dark UI accent; let the photography carry warmth.
- Don't: ship a hero that depends on a third-party video with no poster fallback — at capture the player's error text sat above the H1.

**Open question:** the failed hero video, 3 h1s and unlabelled form controls (audit) are real defects; the craft and restraint justify the nominee prior, confidence medium.
