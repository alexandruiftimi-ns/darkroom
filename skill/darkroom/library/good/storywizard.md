# Story Wizard (story widget for websites) — good

**Thesis in one line:** "Stories for your Website": a clean coral SaaS page where the product itself, a story bubble, floats live on the page.

**Split:** design good · build mixed · technique good — clear, tidy and on-message; a stock kit with contrast gaps.

**First frame:** pale pink-to-white ground; left, a pill badge, a two-line bold claim with "Website." in coral (x 7–45 %, y 34–51 %), two sentences, a coral and a white button, two check-mark reassurances; right, a small node diagram (Create → story circle ← Embed); at the right edge a round story bubble of the product's own widget. The eye goes claim → coral word → button.

## What works & why

- **The product runs on its own landing page** — the floating story bubble is the widget itself → live demo beats description → rule: if the product is a widget, install it on the page.
- **One-sentence promise you can picture** ("Instagram-style stories… in minutes") → rule: name a format the buyer already knows.
- **A real code snippet panel** ("Drop it in. Done in seconds.") → rule: show integration effort as the actual lines.
- **Short sections (46 words max)** → word budget → rule: one idea per section.

## What fails & why

- **Coral and grey small text below 4.5:1** (medium) → contrast → rule: darken the accent for small type.

## Transferable rules

- Do: put the working product on the page.
- Don't: rely on the default kit alone to say who you are; one owned visual would lift it.

## Never copy

Logo, widget art, diagrams and copy. Learn the principle only.
