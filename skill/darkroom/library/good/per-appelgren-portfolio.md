# Per Appelgren portfolio (Shakuro) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a fashion photographer's site where the pictures are the layout — white ground, frames of different sizes, one sans.
- **First frame:** (shot 1) a centred intro paragraph at ~33–65 % x / 12–22 % y, overlapped by a central magazine-cover image with a white arrow button; smaller frames bleed off left and right edges at different heights. Shot 2 (tilted overview): contact strip, client-campaign frames, an about block with a small portrait.

## What works & why
- **Frames at varied sizes and heights, bleeding off the edges** → principle: cadence by scale change (attention.md) → rule: in a photo portfolio, vary frame scale and position instead of a uniform grid.
- **UI reduced to one sans and a white arrow button** → principle: separate UI palette from media palette (color.md rule 1) → rule: when the work is colourful, the interface must be ink and paper.
- **One central frame per viewport, smaller ones at the margins** → principle: one focal point per viewport (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: rank images by size so the eye knows which to open first.
## Transferable rules
- Do: let the photographs supply all colour and display type.
- Don't: let the intro paragraph run under the cover image — its last line is cut off in view (reading-paths.md rule 7).
