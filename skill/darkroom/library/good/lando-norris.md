# Lando Norris — good

**Thesis in one line:** A racing driver sold as a personal brand: his helmet livery colour, his photographs and two-word chapter titles carry everything.

**First frame:** off-white ground with a faint topographic line map (WebGL) and the wordmark top-left; the store pill top-right in the acid-yellow livery. The map draws in over the first second, then the driver photography takes over as you scroll.

## What works & why

- **Acid-yellow from the helmet is the only accent; the rest is off-white, black and photos** → one accent with one owner (color.md) → rule: take the accent from the subject's own object and use it nowhere else.
- **Chapter titles are word pairs at display size (ON TRACK / OFF TRACK) with a handwritten word laid across them** → display type and image as one object (LESSONS) → rule: a personal brand can be told in paired two-word chapters; the script overlay adds the person's hand.
- **Real photography of the person at full bleed between chapters** → show the thing sold → rule: for a personal brand the person is the product; never replace him with illustration.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Phone nav labels are about 17 px tall** (medium) → targets ≥ 24 px (audit) → rule: keep the style, raise the hit area with padding.

## Transferable rules

- Do: pair-word chapters, one livery accent, person-first photography.
- Don't: shrink tap targets to keep a thin nav.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
