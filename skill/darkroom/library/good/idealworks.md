## What works & why
- **One family in two weights for everything** → principle: typography.md budget (2 families, few sizes; weight as the one emphasis device) → rule: a technical B2B brand can run on one disciplined grotesk.
- **Product layers as an accordion beside a single photo, one layer open** → principle: one focal point per viewport (reading-paths.md rule 7); detail on demand (attention.md "How to cut copy" step 3) → rule: show a stack of offerings as a list with one expanded item, not three equal cards.
- **Consent dialog with Deny as large and as yellow as Allow all** → principle: compliance — no dark patterns, equal-weight refusal (GDPR / EDPB consent guidance) → rule: give "Deny" the same size and colour as "Accept".

## Transferable rules
- Do: keep the industrial accent for state and checks only (color.md: one accent).
- Don't: lay dark text on translucent cards over a dark photo — the benefit cards read at near-zero contrast (color.md, WCAG 1.4.3).
