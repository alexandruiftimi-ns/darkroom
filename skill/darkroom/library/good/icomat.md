## What works & why

- **Macro-to-micro scale changes and exploded views of the material** → show the product at its most interesting scale → rule: for B2B hardware, the close-up is the hero.
- **A dot-matrix display font as the one quirk** → one expressive face (typography.md) → rule: one character font for numbers/labels, a calm grotesk for the rest.
- **Plain claims ("We don't sell machines.")** → specific copy → rule: say what you are not; it sharpens what you are.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Grey text on black too faint in places** (medium) → contrast → rule: dark cinematic pages still need ≥ 4.5:1 for body text.

## Transferable rules

- Do: industrial close-ups + one quirky label font.
- Don't: drop body text to dim grey on black.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
