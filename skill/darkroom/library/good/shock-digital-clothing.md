# Shock (HALO LAB concept) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a digital-clothing label sold as a fashion poster — a tile mosaic of model, award and 3D object beside a three-line caps claim.
- **First frame:** left half a 2-column stack of rounded tiles (model photo, mint award tile, chrome blob in a circle); caps headline at ~52–88 % x / 28–50 % y, CTA pill under it, two numbered story rows lower right. Eye: headline → WEAR NOW → numbered rows.

## What works & why
- **Mosaic column vs. type column** — imagery owns the left half, all copy the right → principle: one focal point, image and text zones don't compete (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: split the hero into an image field and a type field instead of laying copy over a collage.
- **CTA directly under the headline (pill + arrow + texture swatch)** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: the first action sits immediately after the claim, before any secondary content.
- **One accent (mint) on one tile** in a grey/navy page → principle: disciplined mode, one accent hue (color.md) → rule: let a single coloured tile carry the brand colour; keep chrome neutral.
## Transferable rules
- Do: give a collage hero its own column and keep the claim in a clean type column.
- Don't: spread the accent across several tiles; one tile reads as signal, three as decoration.

**Open question:** the headline is a caps sentence over 3 lines (tells.md Type); it holds at mockup scale, unverified at mobile widths.
