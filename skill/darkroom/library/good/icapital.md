## What works & why
- **One landscape + one centred line per viewport, 214 words in all** → principle: attention.md budgets and cadence (each viewport a new image/light) → rule: when the offer is abstract, carry it with one metaphor and give each scene a single sentence.
- **Progress rail from mountain to moon** → principle: reading-paths.md rule 5 (corners/edges are for chrome) → rule: a story-scroll needs a visible position indicator in the margin, not in the content.
- **Light changes across the page (day → golden hour → dusk → night)** → principle: cadence hook "light / grade change" (attention.md) → rule: grade a sequence of photos as one day so the scroll itself feels like time passing.

## Transferable rules
- Do: keep the contact action fixed in chrome when the page is all narrative.
- Don't: put a serif italic accent word in every headline (tells.md Type), and don't let transition frames wash text out — mid-fade, the logo and a headline vanished into a bright sky (color.md contrast).
