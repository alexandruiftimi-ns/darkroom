# Villa Aurelia — good

**First frame:** gilt birds (x 50 %, y 30 %) above script "La dolce vita" (y 52 %), season pill below (y 75 %); the eye drops straight down one centred axis in under a second.

## What works & why

- **Script word + three gilt birds on near-black** — one focal object, nothing in the corners → principle: reading-paths.md rule 7 (one focal point) + attention.md hero ≤ 30 words → rule: a luxury hero may be one phrase and one small crafted object; let the dark field do the rest.
- **Season pill (summer/winter) as the only first-frame control** — interaction that changes the offer, not a booking push → principle: reading-paths.md rule 6 (CTA on the path, after the claim) → rule: make the first control a choice about the experience, not a sales button.
- **Dark hero → warm ivory reading body** — the drama is spent once, then a calm serif surface carries the text → principle: color.md disciplined mode (one warm hue family, 3 % accent) → rule: let the hero be the only dark/dramatic frame; read on light.
- **Serif headline with the second line in italic, every section** — one emphasis device repeated → principle: typography.md "one emphasis device per page family" → rule: pick italic or colour or weight for emphasis and repeat it.
- **Script wordmark + birds return in the footer** — bookend motif closes the story → principle: attention.md cadence (repeat as payoff, not filler) → rule: close on the opening motif.

## What fails & why

- **4 families and 23 sizes** — script, two serifs and a sans compete below the hero → principle: typography.md budget (2 families, 5–6 sizes) → rule: one display + one text face; a script only inside the logo/hero art.
- **Blank ivory band on mobile at 75 % and a 128 px crest header** — failed reveal plus a header taking 14 % of every viewport → principle: tells.md "reveals that fail and leave blank bands" → rule: content must render without the reveal firing; shrink sticky chrome after scroll.
- **Stock-glossy wedding and food photos; 255-word Experiences block** — imagery and copy drop below the hero's art direction → principle: attention.md chapter ≤ 45 words → rule: the body must be art-directed as hard as the hero.

## Transferable rules

- Do: one script phrase + one crafted object as a luxury hero; read the rest on a light serif surface.
- Do: make the first interactive control a meaningful choice (season, room, mood).
- Don't: let script, two serifs and a sans all render; don't ship reveals that can leave blank viewports.

## Never copy

Logo, photography, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
