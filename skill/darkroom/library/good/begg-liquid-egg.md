# BEGG (tubik concept) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a commodity (liquid egg in a carton) made into a brand by letting the packaging art direct the whole page.
- **First frame:** right-aligned condensed caps headline at ~20–50 % x / 25–50 % y, a black brush-script line crossing its last word; three yolk-yellow cartons in soft studio light at right; a liquid "spill" edge leads into a nutrition section (big yellow numbers, mono spec table).

## What works & why
- **Brush script lifted from the carton illustration** → principle: handwriting counts as a font and must belong to the artwork (typography.md) → rule: if a script appears in UI, take it from the product's own packaging, not from a font menu.
- **Yellow accent = the product's colour, used for numbers only** → principle: name colour by job, one accent (color.md) → rule: let the product's signature colour mark data (calories, price), not decoration.
- **Spill-shaped section edge into the nutrition block** → principle: cadence hook by shape/colour change (attention.md) → rule: one bespoke section transition derived from the product beats generic dividers.
## Transferable rules
- Do: build the type and colour system from the packaging when the packaging is the brand.
- Don't: let the white display caps sit over light photo areas — the third line already fades into the cream ground (color.md large-text 3:1).

**Open question:** white caps on cream are near the large-text contrast limit in the mockup; a live build must re-check contrast at every crop.
