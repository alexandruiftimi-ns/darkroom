# Agenda (OOZE concept) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a productivity app that shows itself in pieces — slot picker, team update, video — under a huge lowercase claim.
- **First frame:** two-line lowercase headline centred at ~25–40 % y spanning ~45 % of the width; two-line subline; a row of five varied tiles (dark slot picker, photo, mint quote, lilac events, photo with play) with the black "Try it now" button set into the row's centre.
## Signals
| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | 1 family — grotesque sans; display ~150 px · tile titles ~28 px · body ~18 px; ~4 sizes |
| Colour · words · weight | disciplined — white ground, black ink; mint and lilac only inside tiles; ~50 words in frame; weight n/a (mockup) |
| Zones · motion · mobile | desktop 3 · mobile not shown; no motion visible · source: estimated from images |
## What works & why
- **Real UI fragments instead of feature icons** → principle: avoid the "three icon cards" and "fake dashboard" tells by showing substance (tells.md Structure) → rule: show three to five actual product states rather than describing features.
- **Lowercase display at ~150 px, one family** → principle: few sizes, far apart (typography.md Scale) → rule: one confident size jump beats several mid sizes.
- **CTA set into the centre of the tile row** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: put the action where the eye lands after the claim, not only in the nav.
## Transferable rules
- Do: tint each product fragment differently so the row reads as five states of one product.
- Don't: label the same action differently in nav and hero ("Sign up" vs "Try it now").

**Open question:** the skeleton is still centred hero + subline + product strip (tells.md Structure); it escapes the template through scale and real UI, not through layout.
