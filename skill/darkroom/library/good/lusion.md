# Lusion — good

**Thesis in one line:** A 3D studio that proves its craft with one physics toy (blue, black and white shapes you can push) under one plain sentence.

**First frame:** white ground, a two-line statement top-left, then a large rounded panel full of 3D cross shapes in blue, black and white that react to the pointer. "Scroll to explore" sits at the bottom.

## What works & why

- **One interactive object, one sentence** → one focal point (reading-paths.md) → rule: a studio site can sell with a single tactile demo instead of a reel.
- **One blue among black and white** → one accent (color.md) → rule: in 3D, limit materials to two neutrals and one colour.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Looping animations ignore reduced motion** (medium) → motion.md → rule: pause idle loops when the visitor asks for less motion.

## Transferable rules

- Do: one tactile 3D toy + one sentence.
- Don't: keep loops running for reduced-motion visitors.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
