# Monetone annual report (Sebo) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a financial year told as a magazine — the year itself as the hero numeral, then every figure paired with a photo.
- **First frame:** "2022" set at ~45 % of the viewport height across the full content width; under it a 2-column row: headline left, short paragraph + "Scroll down to discover" link right; a yellow stat panel (150 / 9.5b) beside a large team photo; second frame: black chart band with one yellow bar, then a figure/photo checkerboard.

## What works & why
- **The year as the hero image** → principle: one focal point, idea from the subject (reading-paths.md rule 7; tells.md) → rule: in a report, make the defining number the hero instead of a stock photo.
- **Yellow marks exactly one data point per view** (the current year's bar, the key stat panel) → principle: name colour by job (color.md rule 2) → rule: reserve the accent for "this is the number that matters".
- **Figure + photo checkerboard** → principle: related items with their labels in one grid (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: alternate number and image cells so each figure gets a human context.
## Transferable rules
- Do: one sans, extreme size contrast, one accent bound to the key figure.
- Don't: leave lorem-ipsum captions under the figures — visible in the shot (tells.md Copy, placeholders).

**Open question:** stock-style team photos weaken the editorial voice; numbers are invented for the concept (tells.md Proof).
