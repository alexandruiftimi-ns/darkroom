# Pangram Pangram Foundry — good

**Thesis in one line:** A font shop that shows every typeface in a real context photo before you see a specimen.

## What works & why

- **Each font is shown set over a scene that fits its mood** → show the thing sold in use → rule: show the product doing its job before showing it alone.
- **Specimen grid with equal tiles** → one card anatomy (LESSONS).

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **A cookie bar stays over the lower screen until dismissed** (low–medium) → one overlay at a time (tells.md) → rule: keep the consent bar compact.

## Transferable rules

- Do: product in context first, specimen second.
- Don't: let the consent bar cover the product.

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
