# FORUM Cases — good

**Thesis in one line:** rifle cases recast as classical luxury — a chariot crest, Roman-serif product names and dusk field footage instead of tactical clutter.

**First frame:** full-bleed POV video (rifle over dry grass); centred stack at ~50 %, 35–72 %: line-art chariot crest → 3-word tracked caps headline → 14-word line → one "Get early access" button; scroll arrow below. Eye goes crest → headline → button in one vertical drop.

| Source of values | measured (study.json); extra shots of product page |

## What works & why
- **95 words on the whole home page** → principle: attention.md budgets (headline ≤ 8 words, hero ≤ 30) → rule: a three-product shop can sell with a claim, a range and a sign-up; detail lives on product pages.
- **Range row: render, spaced serif name, caps type label, price — identical for all three** → principle: related items in one row, labels in a fixed position (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: give each product a proper name and show name + use + price in the same stack.
- **Product page: media left, name/price/CTA/spec accordions right, story tabs below** → principle: CTA on the path, right after the claim (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: price and the buy action sit directly under the product name; specs fold away.

## Transferable rules
- Do: borrow a mythology (names, crest, serif) to lift a utilitarian object — then keep the grid strict.
- Don't: reveal blocks on scroll without a fallback — the newsletter band was a near-blank fade at capture (tells.md Motion); and don't track caps on every label (typography.md: labels only, ≤ 4 words).

**Open question:** 3 families, tracked-caps overuse and analytics before consent (audit) keep confidence at medium.
