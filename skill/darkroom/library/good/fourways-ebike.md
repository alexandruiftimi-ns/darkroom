# FourWays e-bike (Conceptzilla concept) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** an e-bike launch told in greyscale photography where the only colour is a hot-pink handwritten word.
- **First frame:** (tilted multi-screen composite) dark hero with rider photo, two-line sans claim plus pink script word left of centre, primary pill + text link under it; the product screen isolates the bike on white with colour swatches and a price button.

## What works & why
- **Greyscale product + one pink script word** → principle: monochrome mode, ink + paper + one accent (color.md) → rule: in a monochrome product page spend the accent on one word per section, nothing else.
- **Isolated bike with swatches beside the price button** → principle: related items together, CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rules 4, 6) → rule: put configuration and the buy action in the same cluster as the product shot.
- **Close-up strip (wheels, rear light) under the hero** → principle: cadence by scale/image change (attention.md) → rule: follow a wide hero with a row of close-ups.
## Transferable rules
- Do: keep the product photographic and colourless so the accent and the price carry the hierarchy.
- Don't: set more than one word in the script; two script lines read as decoration.

**Open question:** the script accent word is close to the "accent word inside the headline" tell (tells.md Type); confidence low because only a tilted composite is shown and the reading path can't be verified.
