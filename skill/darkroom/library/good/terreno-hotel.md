# Terreno Barrio Hotel — good

**Thesis in one line:** a hotel that sells the neighbourhood, not the thread count — colour fields and poster caps instead of beige luxury.

**First frame:** inset full-width facade photo inside a lime frame; condensed caps headline centred at ~50 % x / 70 % y, booking bar directly below it; the eye goes headline → booking bar → scroll cue in one vertical drop.

## What works & why

- **Colour field per chapter** — lime hero, red rooms, oxblood barrio/food, sage footer → every viewport changes light without extra motion → principle: cadence hook "colour-field change" (attention.md), expressive mode with one dominant per viewport (color.md) → rule: in an expressive palette give each chapter one field and keep text in that field's own family.
- **Booking bar under the headline, docked on mobile** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: for transactional hospitality, the booking control is the hero's second zone, not a nav button.
- **Rooms in a 2-up grid, photo then caps name, identical alignment** → principle: related items in one row with their labels (reading-paths.md rule 4) → rule: comparable products share one grid and one label position.
- **Circular BOOK NOW stamp with mascot in the corner** → principle: corners are for chrome (rule 5) → rule: one character device may live in chrome; content stays on the axis.
- **Giant script wordmark as the footer image** → principle: one focal point per viewport → rule: end on the brand mark at display scale instead of another text block.

## What fails & why

- **White condensed headline over a white facade** — 1.08:1 measured; the claim disappears into the building → principle: large text ≥ 3:1 (color.md) → rule: place hero copy on the photo's darkest calm area or add a local scrim; check the real crop at every breakpoint.
- **4 families, 13 sizes** → principle: 2 families + utility, 5–6 sizes (typography.md) → rule: a script logo is artwork; don't also add an extended sans for labels.
- **Interruptions** — membership modal over the footer, mobile booking bar covering room titles, trackers before consent (audit) → principle: one focal point; consent-gate third parties → rule: docked bars need bottom padding on content; no modal on scroll-end.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let section colour fields carry cadence; keep the booking/primary action docked and on the path.
- Do: present comparable items in one grid with labels in a fixed position.
- Don't: set white display type over a light photo region; don't stack script + extended + condensed + serif.

## Never copy

Logo, mascot, photography, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.

**Open question:** nominee without an award; confidence medium because the colour/cadence system is strong but the hero contrast and font budget are real defects.
