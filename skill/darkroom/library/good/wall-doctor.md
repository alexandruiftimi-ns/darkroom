# The Wall Doctor (Bellevue contractor) — good

**Thesis in one line:** A local contractor page that says who, where, what and how to call in the first screen, with proof directly under it.
**Split:** design good · build good · technique mixed — clear and trustworthy; no motion idea to borrow.

**First frame:** a dimmed kitchen photo under a two-line serif headline naming the city and the trade (x 8–60 %, y 18–32 %), a three-line description, a red "free estimate" button with a 4.8-star rating beside it; a strip of four trade credentials across the bottom of the hero; phone number and red "Get Quote" top-right. The eye: headline → button → credentials.

## What works & why

- **Headline names city + trade + service** → one h1 that names the offer (tells.md, no who/what) → rule: a local business headline is "<place> <trade>", not a slogan.
- **Phone and quote button always in the header** → task first → rule: the one money action never leaves the screen.
- **Credentials strip under the hero** (BBB, builders' association, Houzz, state licence) → traceable proof (tells.md, Proof) → rule: show checkable badges, not invented numbers.
- **Red used only on actions** → colour by job (references/colour) → rule: one action colour, nothing decorative in it.
- **Service area map and business hours on the page** → local search intent → rule: answer "do you come to me, and when" without a click.

## What fails & why

- **Long service and review blocks** (medium) → word budget → rule: trim each section to its first two lines and a link.

## Transferable rules

- Do: put place, trade, phone and proof in the first screen of any local-service page.
- Don't: mistake this for a look to copy; it is a structure to copy.

## Never copy

Logo, photography, layout and copy. Learn the principle only.
