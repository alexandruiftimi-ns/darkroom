- **One typewriter/mono family from wordmark to body** → principle: tone matches the brand; fewer families, few sizes (typography.md) → rule: a characterful utility face used everywhere at 3–4 sizes can replace a display + text pair.
- **Material close-ups as heroes (suede weave, sandals on straw)** → principle: one focal point per viewport (reading-paths.md rule 7); cadence by image change (attention.md) → rule: for craft goods, make the texture the full-bleed image and keep copy to a single link.
