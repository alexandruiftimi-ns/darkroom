# Later — Made You Look — good
- **Thesis in one line:** an influencer-marketing campaign page that sells attention by staging each creator as an editorial spread between two grainy gradient bookends.
- **First frame:** desktop opens on the loader (thumb icon, "Loading 98%") centred on a pink-red-violet grain field; mobile shows the 4-line claim centred at ~50 % / 45 % with a play button on top of it; consent panel covers the bottom third in every view.

## What works & why
- **Fixed chapter kit per creator** — giant vertical gradient name, one video, scattered photos, one pull quote, short bio → principle: cadence by repeated structure, one focal point per viewport (attention.md; reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: a showcase gives every featured person the same kit and varies only the content.
- **The creator's quote is the display type** — heaviest text on each chapter, gradient quote mark as its only colour → principle: tone matches the brand's voice (typography.md); accent with one job (color.md) → rule: in testimonial-led pages, set the quote at display size and drop the section headline.
- **Gradient grain only on the bookends**, closing on a centred claim and one black angled CTA → principle: separate media palette from UI palette (color.md); CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: expressive colour may live in backgrounds if the UI stays black-on-cream with one CTA style.
## Transferable rules
- Do: one chapter kit, repeated; quotes at display size; one CTA style everywhere.
- Don't: spread bio, photos and quote to all four corners (desktop p025/p075) — the eye orbits.

**Open question:** loader and consent panel hide the true first frame, body copy is pushed into corner columns, and small creator labels measured 1.11:1 over imagery (audit) — nominee prior kept, confidence medium.
