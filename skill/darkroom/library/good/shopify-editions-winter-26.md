# Shopify Editions Winter '26 — good

**Thesis in one line:** A 100-feature product release held together by one borrowed visual world: Renaissance paintings mixed with real product screens.

**First frame:** a Renaissance painting of figures with a skateboard and product UI cards layered on it, a small serif heading at left.

## What works & why

- **One art-historical visual system for every chapter** → a consistent world (looks.md) → rule: a very long launch page needs one borrowed visual world that every chapter reuses.
- **Huge serif chapter words (Marketing) over painted landscapes, with real UI screenshots inside them** → display type and image as one object → rule: keep the product real inside the fantasy.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Very long and slow on phones (about 6.6 s to load, measured by scrollytelling.ai)** (medium) → page weight → rule: lazy-load chapters past the second screen.

## Transferable rules

- Do: one borrowed art world across all chapters; real UI inside it.
- Don't: load every chapter up front.
