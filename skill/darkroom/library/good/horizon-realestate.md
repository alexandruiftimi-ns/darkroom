# Horizon (real-estate template) — good

**Thesis in one line:** the brand name is the landscape: HORIZON in thin geometric caps where the O is a half-sun setting behind the line.
**Split:** design good · build mixed · technique good — wordmark-as-image idea; template scroll and overlap bugs.

**First frame:** pale grey ground; the wordmark spans 3–96 % x at 23–52 % y in thin black geometric caps; the O drops below the baseline as an arch and an orange gradient sun dot hangs above it; a mountain-and-forest photo fills the lower 40 %; small caps nav top-right with a black pill "Contact".

## What works & why

- **The name drawn as a sunset** → a logo that carries the subject (craft) → rule: give the wordmark one letter that becomes the picture.
- **One orange dot as the only colour** → single accent with a meaning (colour.md) → rule: the accent belongs to the brand idea, then reappears small (nav dot, footer).
- **Wordmark opens and closes the page** (wordmark-bookend) → rule: bookend a template with the mark, not another link block.
- **Numbered amenity list with distances** → scannable proof → rule: real estate sells the neighbourhood as a numbered list.

## What fails & why

- **Grey card copy at ≈ 2.4:1** (medium) → contrast law → rule: description text ≥ 4.5:1.
- **Scroll and overlap bugs** (medium, per the thread) → rule: test sticky and overlapping sections at every breakpoint.

## Transferable rules

- Do: make the brand mark itself the hero image.
- Don't: let template grey text fall under contrast.

## Never copy

Logo, wordmark drawing, photography, layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
