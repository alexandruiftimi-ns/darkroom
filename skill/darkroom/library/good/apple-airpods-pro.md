## What works & why

- **One claim per screen at display size** → one focal point per viewport → rule: a product page is a sequence of single claims, each with its own image.
- **Apple has moved its famous scroll reveals from image sequences to short videos** → frames vs video (recipes/README.md) → rule: try video first; use frames only when exact reversible control matters.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Very long (4,000+ words) further down** (low for Apple, high for a small brand) → word budgets → rule: copy Apple's first five screens, not its spec sheet.

## Transferable rules

- Do: one claim per screen; video before frames.
- Don't: copy the spec-sheet length.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
