# Cominvi — good

**Thesis in one line:** an underground-mining contractor that looks as engineered as its machines — cinematic cave first, Swiss-grid proof after.

**First frame:** dark blue cave render, lit truck right of centre (~70 % x, 52 % y); two-line white grotesk H1 at the left gutter (~2–42 % x, 47–56 % y); a mono stat pinned beside the headlight; two image link cards bottom-right. The eye goes headline → truck/stat → cards.

## What works & why

- **Claim left, subject right, one stat on the subject** → principle: text zone and focal point separate (reading-paths.md rule 7); concrete fact over adjectives (attention.md) → rule: pin the one proof number beside the thing it measures, not in a stat bar.
- **Grotesk + mono with strict roles** — mono only for figures, units, section indices (S.03 MINERALS) → principle: utility face for real data (typography.md) → rule: a mono earns its place by labelling numbers and indices, nothing else.
- **Dark graded hero → light sage proof sections** → principle: cadence hook "light change" (attention.md) → rule: open in atmosphere, switch to a light ground when the page turns to evidence.
- **Mineral dial: one specimen, active name black, others ~15 % grey** → principle: one focal point; related items in one column (reading-paths.md rules 4, 7) → rule: show comparable items as one list + one large exemplar instead of a thumbnail grid.
- **Stats as a 2×3 hairline grid** (icon, big number, mono caption) → principle: proof as ≤ 5–6 short items (attention.md) → rule: numbers carry B2B proof; give each a unit label in the utility face.

## What fails & why

- **Copy weight** — technology 215 words, opening with a 5-line paragraph at display size; services 117, about 94 → principle: chapter budget ≤ 45 words (attention.md) → rule: one sentence at display size; the rest at body size or behind a link.
- **12 font sizes** → principle: 5–6 sizes, far apart (typography.md) → rule: collapse near-duplicates (11/12/13/14, 20/24) into one step each.
- **CTA labels flagged 1:1 and 3 links without names** (likely hover-duplicate text — verify) → principle: WCAG 1.4.3, 4.1.2 → rule: CTA text visible at rest and every link named.

## Transferable rules

- Do: cinematic hero for emotion, light grid for proof; pin one stat to the subject.
- Do: use a mono only for numbers and section indices.
- Don't: set paragraphs at display size; don't let type sizes multiply past six.

## Never copy

Logo, renders, photography, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
