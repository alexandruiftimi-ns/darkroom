# Givenchy concept (LAIN) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a fashion house as a black editorial page — an ultra-condensed wordmark that the portrait cuts into.
- **First frame:** wordmark fills the top ~30 % of the page, last letters replaced by a portrait in white tailoring; a small caps paragraph under it at left; below, offset photos, two huge asterisks and a stacked "20/24" numeral; a narrow-column variant beside it repeats the system.
## Signals
| Signal | Value |
|---|---|
| Type | 2 families — ultra-condensed grotesque caps (display ~350 px) · extended grotesque (labels/body ~12 px); ~5 sizes |
| Colour · words · weight | monochrome — black ground, white ink, colour only in photos; ~120 words visible; weight n/a (mockup) |
| Zones · motion · mobile | desktop 3 · narrow-column variant shown (not verified as a mobile breakpoint); no motion visible · source: estimated from images |
## What works & why
- **Portrait replacing part of the wordmark** → principle: one focal point where type and image are one unit (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: let the hero image interrupt the display word instead of sitting beside it.
- **One display face used for every large element** (wordmark, numerals, statement) → principle: one expressive face + one text face (typography.md) → rule: a luxury voice comes from repeating one display face at extreme scale, not from mixing.
- **Black ground with colour only in photography** → principle: monochrome mode (color.md) → rule: in fashion, keep chrome ink-and-paper and let the garments carry colour.
## Transferable rules
- Do: crop the wordmark with the hero image; keep everything else ink and paper.
- Don't: set body copy in title case at ~12 px in white on black — it is the weakest part of the view (typography.md details).

**Open question:** uses a real house's name without affiliation; learn the system only. The asterisk pair is decorative and repeats on both frames.
