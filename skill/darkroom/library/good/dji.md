## What works & why

- **Footage shot by the product is the hero** → show what the product makes → rule: if the product creates images, its output is the best hero.
- **Clean product cut-outs in an even grid** → one card anatomy (LESSONS).
- **Categories by use (video production, agriculture, enterprise)** → navigate by task → rule: group a big catalogue by what people do.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Wide carousels can push the phone layout sideways** (medium) → overflow → rule: check the 390 px layout for horizontal scroll.

## Transferable rules

- Do: product-made footage as hero; categories by task.
- Don't: let wide carousels push the phone page sideways.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
