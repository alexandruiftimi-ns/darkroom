# Grand Emily Hotel (Afterglow) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** the atrium is the product — one full-bleed interior photograph, one serif title, one booking line.
- **First frame:** warm dark lobby photo edge to edge; spaced serif caps title centred at ~50 % y; directly under it an underline-only booking bar (dates, rooms, guests, LET'S GO); thin top bar with menu, centred wordmark, locations and BOOK NOW; a two-line description across the bottom.

## What works & why
- **Booking bar immediately under the title** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: for hotels, the date/room control is the hero's second line, not a modal.
- **Underline-only fields and a ghost button over the photo** → principle: separate UI palette from media palette (color.md rule 1) → rule: on a rich photo, draw UI as hairlines in one ink so it doesn't fight the image.
- **Title placed on the calm dark band of the photo** → principle: focal point and text zone don't compete (reading-paths.md rule 7) → rule: pick the crop so the headline lands on the darkest calm area.
## Transferable rules
- Do: one photo, one title, one booking line — let the interior carry the luxury.
- Don't: set the bottom description at ~14 px grey over a busy carpet; legibility drops (color.md 4.5:1).

**Open question:** body text and booking labels over the photo need a scrim on a live build; confidence medium because the poster frame is the only state inspected.
