- **Three-word claim spread across the frame width**, ≤ 40 words below it and one quiet "Learn more" → principle: headline budget ≤ 8 words (attention.md); CTA after the claim (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: a luxury section is one short claim, one short paragraph, one link.
- **Serif display + tiny caps utility, zero UI colour** → principle: 2 families with distinct roles (typography.md); disciplined mode (color.md) → rule: let the photography own all colour; UI is ink plus one neutral.
