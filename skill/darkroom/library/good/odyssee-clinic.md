# Odyssée Clinic — good

**Thesis in one line:** a medical-aesthetics clinic sold as calm skin and science — full-bleed portrait video, a serif wordmark and almost no words.

**First frame:** full-bleed soft portrait (face top-centre, hands mid-frame); one horizontal band at ~52 % y carries wordmark (left), a 5-word serif claim (~18–36 % x) and four nav links (right). The eye lands on the face, drops to the band, reads it left to right.

## What works & why

- **Indented-first-line serif quote in the right half, caps label far left** — the same anchor in philosophy, approach and footer → principle: one copy anchor (reading-paths.md rule 1), typography "built, not assembled" details → rule: pick one editorial device (indent, label column) and repeat it for every statement section.
- **Faded carousel with one active card + "3 : 4" counter** — four benefits read one at a time → principle: one focal point per viewport (rule 7), cadence (attention.md) → rule: when items are equal, light one and dim the rest instead of showing four at equal weight.
- **UI has no accent hue; skin tones are the only colour** → principle: separate UI palette from media palette (color.md) → rule: in luxury-care, let photography carry warmth and keep the UI to ground + one ink.
- **Footer ends on a single pearl object with the wordmark** → principle: one focal point; end on the brand mark → rule: close with one object-image, not another link grid.

## What fails & why

- **White hero copy and nav over a pale skin/grey video** — measured ~1.0:1 against the frame behind; legibility depends on the video frame → principle: large text ≥ 3:1 (color.md) → rule: test overlay text against the lightest video frame, add a local scrim or switch ink.
- **Carousel's dimmed cards at ~10 % opacity and grey legal links** — titles of three of four items are effectively invisible; legal links low contrast → principle: contrast pairs ≥ 4.5:1 (color.md) → rule: dim inactive items by size or blur, not to unreadable opacity.
- **Audit: 2 h1, 9 images without alt, trackers before consent** → principle: semantic structure and consent-gating (tells.md "Function") → rule: the calm surface needs real semantics underneath.

## Transferable rules

- Do: put the hero's wordmark, claim and nav in one horizontal band across the image; one reading line.
- Do: repeat one typographic device (indented serif quote + caps label) as the section system.
- Don't: rely on a soft pale video to carry white text.

## Never copy

Logo, photography, pearl render, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.

**Open question:** jury praised the craft; confidence medium because hero contrast and dimmed-card legibility are measurable defects.
