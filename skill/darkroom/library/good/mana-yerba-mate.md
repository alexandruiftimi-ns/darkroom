## What works & why

- **The can stays pinned while big questions scroll by (MANA? YERBA MATÉ? WHAT ARE WE TALKING ABOUT?)** → one fixed focal object, moving copy (motion-craft.md) → rule: pin the packshot and let the chapter copy travel past it.
- **Each flavour owns its background colour in the product row** → colour with a named owner (color.md) → rule: in a catalog of few products, give each product a colour field and keep the UI neutral.
- **Illustration is original and everywhere, including a footer character you can make jump** → personality through craft → rule: one playful interaction at the end rewards a full scroll.
- **167 words on the whole page** → word budgets (attention.md) → rule: a drink sells on taste and mood, not paragraphs.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Ad trackers load before consent** (medium, legal) → comply.md → rule: gate marketing tags behind consent.

## Transferable rules

- Do: pinned packshot + chapter questions; one colour per product.
- Don't: load ad pixels before the visitor agrees.

## Never copy

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
