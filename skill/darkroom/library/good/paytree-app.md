# Paytree (Pandi Atuk) — good
- **Thesis in one line:** a payments app with one concrete promise — one transaction, one tree — staged inside an illustrated forest.
- **First frame:** full illustrated scene (trunks framing left/right, wind turbines on the horizon, owl on a branch); two-line slab-serif headline centred at ~28–37 % y, question subline, single App Store button; the phone rises from the undergrowth at ~50 % x, two annotated features left and right of it.

## What works & why
- **Headline is a specific, checkable promise** → principle: a headline that couldn't describe a competitor (tells.md Copy) → rule: state the one mechanism that makes the product different, in ≤ 8 words.
- **Single CTA directly under the claim** → principle: CTA on the path (reading-paths.md rule 6) → rule: one platform button beats a row of store badges + "learn more".
- **Phone grows out of the scene's centre with features annotated beside it** → principle: one focal point, labels with their item (reading-paths.md rules 4, 7) → rule: annotate the device directly instead of a separate feature grid.
## Transferable rules
- Do: build one illustrated world around the product's mechanism and keep UI white on top of it.
- Don't: rely on violet as the default "tech" hue — here it is justified by the night-forest scene only (tells.md Colour).

**Open question:** violet palette and centred hero are both registry tells; kept as good for the concrete promise and committed illustration — confidence low.
