# Mahadeva (AI agency template) — bad

**Thesis in one line:** "Custom AI Solutions to Increase Revenue", centred on dark green between falling pastel blocks: the AI-agency starter kit.

**Split:** design bad · build mixed · technique mixed — generic claim and section run; transitions are the only idea.

**First frame:** deep green-black ground; a two-line centred claim (x 30–70 %, y 39–52 %) with one sentence and two outline buttons; pastel green, lilac, blue and yellow rectangles drift down both side margins; a dotted caps nav on top; "Buy Template" and "Made in Framer" badges float bottom-right. The eye goes claim → buttons → badges.

## What fails & why

- **A headline that fits any agency** (high) → generic claim (references/tells, Copy) → rule: the first line names a specific result for a specific buyer.
- **The stock section order** hero → cards → icon grid → testimonials → pricing → CTA (medium) → tells, Structure → rule: order sections by the buyer's questions, not the kit's.
- **Four pastel hues with no owners** (medium) → colour discipline → rule: one accent, or a colour per named thing.
- **Floating promo badges over the page on phones** (medium) → rule: nothing fixed may cover content on a 390 px screen.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let motion carry meaning about the product.
- Don't: ship a template's demo copy and pastel defaults as a brand.

## Never copy

Layout, blocks, copy. Learn the principle only.
