# Naypache Studio — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A web studio that sells packages and FAQs before it shows a single finished site.
**Split:** design mixed · build mixed · technique good — strong monogram idea; no work shown, empty mid-scroll screens.

**First frame:** cream ground; a blocky four-letter monogram splits into two pastel pairs (yellow/peach left, pink/lilac right) around a centred quote whose initial letters pick up the pastels; below, a 40 px "Custom Web Design Agency in Potsdam" line, two lines of grey body and a black "Packages" button bottom-right. The eye: monogram → quote → headline.

## What works & why

- **A monogram that changes colour and arrangement as the page goes** → one device repeated (references/craft) → rule: let the mark itself be the motion and bookend the page with it.

## What fails & why

- **No case studies; a studio with no visible work** (high) → proof beats claims → rule: an agency shows three finished projects before any pricing.
- **Whole screens of empty cream with one small 3D object** (medium) → density / scroll needs a payoff → rule: every full screen carries a message, not just an object.
- **Grey body copy on cream and on black fails contrast** (medium) → contrast → rule: body ≥ 4.5:1 on every ground.
- **Generic quote and stock agency headline** (medium) → generic copy (tells.md) → rule: the first line says what is different about this studio, not what all studios do.
- **900 words of packages, FAQ and insights** (medium) → word budget → rule: move long-form sales copy behind the work.

## Transferable rules

- Do: animate the brand mark rather than decorating around it.
- Don't: sell packages before proof; don't spend whole screens on one floating object.

## Never copy

Monogram, 3D objects, layout and copy. Learn the principle only.
