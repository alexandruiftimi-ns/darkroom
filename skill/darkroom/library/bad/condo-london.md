# Condo London — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A wild illustrated identity where 2,000 words are crammed into a map on noise texture.

**First frame:** an orange poster with a head illustration and hand-drawn type.

## What works & why

- **A bold, original poster identity** → personality.

## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Text set on noisy texture inside a map** (high) → text needs its own ground (tells.md) → rule: put reading text on a calm panel.
- **Four decorative fonts** (medium) → font budget.

## Transferable rules

- Don't: set body text on texture.

Logo, imagery, characters, footage, exact layout and copy text. Learn the principle only.
