# Nitro Studio — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A visual designer's portfolio that shows no visuals: a name, a paragraph and a numbered list whose work appears only on hover.

**Split:** design mixed · build good · technique bad — calm and light, but the work hides behind hover.

**First frame:** warm grain off-white; "NITRO STUDIO" in black grotesk caps at about 120 px top-left; a three-line bio in grey; then a ruled list of five rows (01 AI Videos … 05 Contact) at about 36 px. A black dot cursor sits mid-screen. No image anywhere.

## What works & why

- **Ruled numbered list as the menu** — five rows, mono numerals, generous spacing; easy to scan → one layout primitive → rule: a numbered ruled list is a clean index for a small body of work.

## What fails & why

- **No work in the first frame or anywhere at rest** (high) — a designer of films and visuals shows only text; the images exist only under the pointer → the work is the interface (good/brandon-yasin) → rule: a portfolio's first frame shows at least one piece of work.
- **Hover-only reveal** (medium) — phones have no hover, so phone visitors see a list and logos only; Reddit readers called it not calm → touch has no hover → rule: hover can enlarge, never be the only way to see work.
- **Generic grotesk caps wordmark** (medium) — black caps on off-white with a cropped giant repeat at the bottom; nothing ties it to motion or AI film → the device must come from the subject → rule: give the name one move or image that only this studio would make.

## Transferable rules

- Do: keep the calm index, and pin a still from each project into its row.
- Don't: make hover the only door to the work.

## Never copy

Logo, client marks, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
