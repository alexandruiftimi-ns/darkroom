## What fails & why

_Only issues that cost visitors something (severity in brackets). Minor polish is left out on purpose._

- **Click-to-start gate** (high) → no gate before content (tells.md) → rule: start the experience on scroll; never require a button.
- **Grey text on black fails contrast widely** (medium) → contrast → rule: dark portfolios still need readable text.

## Transferable rules
