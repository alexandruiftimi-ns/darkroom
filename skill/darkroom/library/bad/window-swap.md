# WindowSwap (window views from around the world) — bad

**Thesis in one line:** "Open a window somewhere in the world": a lovely idea whose first screen is a blur and whose page is mostly gift, tip-jar and survey panels.

**Split:** design bad · build bad · technique good — content idea is warm; the page around it is hand-made and retro.

**First frame:** a defocused amber-and-teal video fills the screen (a window, out of focus); one outlined pill with a serif line sits dead centre; a terracotta "buy us a coffee" pill, small nav and a serif wordmark top; below the fold a deep teal band with a hand-drawn gift doodle. The eye has nothing sharp to land on.

## What works & why

- **Real views from real windows** — user footage carries the feeling → rule: content you can't fake is the best hero.

## What fails & why

- **A blurred first frame** (high) — the one thing the site offers (a view) is hidden until you click → hero media → rule: show a sharp frame of the product in the first screen.
- **Hand-drawn doodles, cream serif, terracotta and teal** (medium) → tells (cream + terracotta + serif) · owner taste: not retro or hand-made → rule: warmth from the footage, not from craft-fair styling.
- **Most of the page is asks** (gift, membership, tip jar, survey) (medium) → rule: one support ask, after the experience.
- **No keyboard focus on any control** (medium) → keyboard → rule: visible focus on every link.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let the real content open the page, in focus.
- Don't: stack donation panels where the story should be.

## Never copy

Footage, illustrations, logo and copy. Learn the principle only.

**Open question:** the earlier analysis called it good (warm, human); the owner rated it bad. Owner taste (no retro, no hand-made) decides; confidence lowered.
