## What works & why

- **The phone version is a real page** → mobile re-composition → rule: when the desktop is a 3D world, the phone still gets a plain list of the same doors.

## What fails & why

- **The work is too small to see** (high) → portfolio law: the work is the interface (brandon-yasin) → rule: a portfolio's first frame shows at least one project at readable size.
- **Toy-diorama mood instead of a polished, modern one** (medium) → owner's taste (polished, cinematic) → rule: a world metaphor must look as finished as a product shot, or skip it.
- **The headline lives inside the scene and appears after an intro** (medium) → tells.md loader instead of a first frame → rule: name and role in live text at first paint.

## Transferable rules

- Do: put one real project at full size in the first frame.
- Don't: hide the work inside props.

## Never copy

Name, scene, props, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
