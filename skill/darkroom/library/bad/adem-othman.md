# Adem Othman — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A personal site of pale-orange prose on a pink-white page, opened by a "Hi" splash and decorated with stickers.

**Split:** design mixed · build mixed · technique bad — elegant serif, but washed-out text and quirky decoration.

**First frame:** after a white screen with a large "Hi", a blush-white page; an orange display serif "Greetings." at about 60 px, a wavy hairline with a globe sticker and a tilted orange download sticker with sparkles, then "Profile" and four paragraphs of light peach-grey body with orange links. A slim icon rail left (home, profile, laptop, moon, chat).

## What works & why

- **A real reading column** — one measure, generous leading, a display serif for headings → editorial typesetting → rule: a personal site can be a well-set page of prose.

## What fails & why

- **Washed-out text** (high) — peach-grey body and asides on blush white fall to about 1.7:1 in places → contrast → rule: body text at 4.5:1 or better; colour the links, not the paragraphs.
- **Stickers and sparkles as decoration** (medium) — a tilted download sticker, a globe badge and a wavy rule read hand-made and cute rather than modern → maintainer taste: not quirky → rule: one restrained device, drawn from the subject.
- **"Hi" splash before the page** (medium) — a screen with one word delays the first frame → tells: loader instead of a first frame → rule: say hello in the page, not before it.
- **No work in view** (medium) — four paragraphs of biography before any project → the work is the interface → rule: one project in the first frame.

## Transferable rules

- Do: set prose well, with one display face for headings.
- Don't: tint body text toward the accent until it disappears.

## Never copy

Logo, illustrations, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
