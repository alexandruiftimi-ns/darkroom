# Don't Board Me — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A pet-care brand that makes you bounce a ball before you can see the site.

**First frame:** pink ground, "BOUNCE A BALL TO GET TO THE SITE" in red condensed caps with a yellow ball. Nothing else until you play.
