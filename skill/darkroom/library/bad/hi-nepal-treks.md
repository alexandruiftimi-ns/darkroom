# Hi Nepal Travels & Treks — bad

**Thesis in one line:** A trekking agency with the Himalayas to sell that opens on a generic rafting photo, a search bar and a wall of SEO paragraphs.

**Split:** design bad · build mixed · technique bad — stock tour template; no motion idea; text walls.

**First frame:** green contact bar, white nav with a busy logo, then a dimmed rafting photo with a two-line bold claim (7–56 % x, 37–53 % y), a two-line intro and a grey search field with an orange button; orange chat and WhatsApp bubbles stacked bottom-right. Eye: logo → claim → bubbles; no mountain in sight.

## What fails & why

- **The hero photo is not the product** (high) → thesis in the first frame (references/craft) → rule: a trekking brand opens on the trek: the mountain, the trail, the summit.
- **Justified SEO paragraphs above every card row** (medium) → word budget; justified text rivers → rule: one sentence per section; keep SEO copy below the cards, ragged-right.
- **Two accent colours and two floating bubbles fighting the CTA** (medium) → one accent with a job → rule: one action colour; one help bubble at most.
- **Identical card rows repeated six times** (medium) → tells.md (every block the same card) → rule: vary section shapes: a map, a feature trek, a list.

## Transferable rules

- Do: let the destination's best photograph carry the first frame.
- Don't: stack SEO prose, search and chat widgets above the offer.

## Never copy

Logo, photography, exact layout, copy text. Learn the principle only.
