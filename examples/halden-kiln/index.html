One firing, five stages

20°C
  1. 20° · Day one

    Loaded by hand.

    Each piece is stacked on shelves of its own clay, with space for the flame to pass.

  2. 200° · Hour 6

    The candle burn.

    Slow wood heat drives out the last water. Rush it and the clay cracks.

  3. 900° · Hour 22

    The long climb.

    The clay turns to ceramic. The kiln is fed every few minutes, day and night.

  4. 1,280° · Hour 34

    Salt goes in.

    Salt thrown into the fire vaporises and settles on every surface as glaze.

  5. 400° · Day four

    Closed, and quiet.

    Two days of cooling. Then the door opens and nothing is the same.

What comes out.

  • Salt-glaze bottle 1.2 L
  • Tea bowl 11 cm
  • Dinner plate 27 cm
